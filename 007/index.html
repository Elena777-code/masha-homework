2📚 Your own words — meet them properly

These are your words from our lessons. Say the English aloud first, then click — the translation, what it means, an example. Read them all twice this week. No quiz today — the drill on these comes next time.

сборная
→ the (school) team — She plays for the school volleyball team.
классный руководитель
→ form teacher (British) / homeroom teacher (American) — Our form teacher checks our diaries every morning.
актовый зал
→ (school) assembly hall — The whole school gathered in the assembly hall.
началка
→ primary school — I've known her since primary school.
иероглиф the trap word!
→ Chinese character(s) — She can write a hundred Chinese characters. (hieroglyphs are Egyptian — not Chinese!)
экзаменатор
→ examiner — The examiner asked us to work in pairs.
свидетель
→ witness — a person who sees a crime or an accident happen — The witness saw the robbery from her window.
карманник
→ pickpocket — a thief who steals from people's pockets or bags in a crowd — A pickpocket took my phone on the bus.
аксолотль
→ axolotl — a Mexican water animal that can regrow lost legs — Axolotls can even regrow parts of their heart.
10–11 классы
→ senior high school — In Russia senior high school is grades 10–11; in the UK it's called sixth form. ("higher" in English usually means university!)
директор школы the trap pair!
→ principal — the head of a school — Our principal announced the new rules. A principle is a rule you believe in — It's a matter of principle.
