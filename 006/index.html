5💬 The words you asked about

From Wednesday's lesson — say the English first, then click. These are yours now (fast quiz next time!).

сборная
→ the (school) team — She plays for the school volleyball team.
классный руководитель
→ form teacher (British) / homeroom teacher (American) — Our form teacher checks our diaries every morning.
актовый зал
→ (school) assembly hall — The whole school gathered in the assembly hall.
началка
→ primary school — I've known her since primary school.
иероглиф careful — rr!
→ Chinese character(s) — She can write a hundred Chinese characters. (hieroglyphs are Egyptian!)
экзаменатор
→ examiner — The examiner asked us to work in pairs.
аксолоты
→ axolotls — Axolotls can regrow lost legs — and even parts of their heart.
10–11 классы — это какая школа?
→ senior high school — In Russia senior high school is grades 10–11; in the UK it's Years 12–13, called sixth form. ("higher" in English usually means university!)
principle the school word is different!
→ the head of a school = principal — Our principal announced the new rules. A principle is a rule you believe in — It's a matter of principle.
promotion
→ He got a promotion at work. (in a shop: a special offer — The shop is running a promotion.)
advertisement
→ I saw an advertisement for a new phone. (informal: an ad / adverts)
look after
→ take care of — Can you look after my cat for a week?
