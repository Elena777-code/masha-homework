4🎧 The bilingual video — watch like a pro

The video is in our Telegram. Watch in three steps — and you'll come to the lesson ready to win the debate.

First watch — no pausing. Just answer: what are the three types of bilinguals? Write 1–2–3 in Russian or English.
Second watch — pause when you hear a type. Note ONE fact about each type (age? how many languages? benefit?).
Third watch (or only the parts you need) — decide: which type are YOU? Find one sentence in the video that proves it.

Hard words from the video — just glance at them, nothing to memorise (press if curious):

bilingual
two languages in one brain — двуязычный
benefit
a good thing something gives you — польза
delay
make later, slow down — задерживать (the video says bilingual brains may delay dementia)
dementia
a serious memory illness of old age — деменция (only to understand, not to learn!)
